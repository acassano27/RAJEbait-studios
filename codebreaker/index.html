Codebreaker

Try to match the hidden code by adjusting one digit at a time.

How to play

You are trying to crack a hidden 3-digit code. Use your arrow keys or the on-screen control pad to select and adjust digits. Each digit must be unique--no repeats allowed!

  • ◀ ▶ move between digits
  • ▲ ▼ increase or decrease digit
  • valid digit + correct position
  • valid digit + incorrect position

Example

4 8 2
Hidden code
8 4 2
Your guess
2 8 4
New result

The 2 is exact. The 8 and 4 are valid digits in the code, but they are not in the correct positions.
